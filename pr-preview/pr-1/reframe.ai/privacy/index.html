Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 1, 2026

1. Introduction

AI Solutions UG (haftungsbeschränkt) ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Reframe AI application (the "App") for Shopify merchants. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By installing and using Reframe AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Shopify Store Data

When you install Reframe AI, we collect and process the following data from your Shopify store:

  • Store Information: Your shop domain, store name, and contact email
  • Product Data: Product names, descriptions, and images that you choose to enhance with our AI service
  • Authentication Data: OAuth tokens and session tokens required for secure API access
  • Account Information: Your Shopify account ID and store owner details

2.2 Generated Content

We store and process:

  • Source Images: Original product images you upload or select for AI processing (which may include images containing people or identifiable individuals)
  • Generated Images: AI-enhanced images created by our service (which may also contain people or identifiable individuals from the source images)
  • Prompts and Instructions: Text prompts and editing instructions you provide
  • Image Metadata: Generation timestamp, processing duration, and technical parameters

Important: You are responsible for ensuring you have the necessary rights and consents to use and process any images containing people or identifiable individuals. This includes obtaining appropriate model releases and consent for AI processing and publication.

2.3 Usage and Billing Data

  • Subscription Information: Your current plan (Free, Basic, or Professional)
  • Usage Metrics: Number of images generated, billing period data, and overage usage
  • AI Call Logs: Requests sent to our AI engine, including prompts, token usage, and processing time

2.4 Technical Information

  • Log Data: IP addresses, browser type, access times, and error logs
  • Performance Data: App performance metrics and usage patterns

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Delivery

  • To provide and maintain the Reframe AI service
  • To generate AI-powered product images based on your input
  • To enable you to view, download, and manage generated images
  • To sync generated images back to your Shopify product catalog

3.2 Billing and Account Management

  • To process subscription payments and manage billing periods
  • To track usage against your plan limits and calculate overage charges
  • To send billing notifications and receipts

3.3 Service Improvement

  • To analyze app performance and optimize AI processing
  • To understand usage patterns and improve user experience
  • To troubleshoot technical issues and provide customer support

3.4 Legal Compliance

  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect our rights

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Gemini AI

Reframe AI uses Google's Gemini AI service to generate and enhance product images. When you use our service, your product images and text prompts are sent to Google's AI infrastructure for processing. Google's processing of this data is governed by their privacy policy and terms of service.

Images Containing People: If your source images contain people or identifiable individuals, these images will also be transmitted to Google Gemini for AI processing. The generated images may also contain these individuals. You are solely responsible for ensuring you have obtained all necessary rights, consents, and model releases before processing such images through our service.

Data Minimization: We only send the minimum necessary data (images and prompts) to Google for processing.

4.2 Shopify

Our App operates within the Shopify ecosystem and uses Shopify's APIs to access your store data. Shopify's handling of your store data is governed by their privacy policy.

4.3 Payment Processing

Subscription payments are processed through Shopify's billing system. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.

4.4 Hosting and Infrastructure

Our App is hosted on secure cloud infrastructure. Your data may be stored and processed on servers located in various regions to ensure optimal performance and reliability.

Image Storage: Generated images are stored on Cloudflare R2, a secure object storage service. Cloudflare's handling of stored data is governed by their privacy policy and data processing agreement.

4.5 No Sale of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or generated images to third parties for their marketing purposes.

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Data Storage

  • Database: Store and account data is stored in a PostgreSQL database with encryption at rest
  • Image Storage: Generated images are stored securely and are accessible only to your store
  • Backup: Regular backups are performed to prevent data loss

5.2 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Secure OAuth authentication with Shopify
  • Access controls and authentication requirements
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure handling of API tokens and session data

5.3 Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. When you uninstall the App, your data will be retained for a reasonable period (typically 90 days) to allow for potential reinstallation, after which it will be permanently deleted.
